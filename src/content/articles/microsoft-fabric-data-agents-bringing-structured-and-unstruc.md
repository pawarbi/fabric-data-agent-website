summary: "Most enterprise questions do not live neatly in one place. The numbers may be in a warehouse, the rules may be buried in PDF files, and the explanation may sit in a support document that only three people know exists. A [Microsoft Fabric data agent](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/data-science/concept-data-agent?utm_source=chatgpt.com) gives users a conversational way to ask questions across that information while keeping the answer grounded in approved enterprise data. I think of it as a configurable virtual analyst for a business domain, not a generic chatbot that happens to know a little SQL. Fabric Data Agents are now generally available, while some of the newer data sources, integrations, and advanced capabilities discussed later in this post are still in preview."
