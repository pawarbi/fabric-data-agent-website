summary: "Power BI helps people understand their data and act on it. With AI, what’s changing is where trusted analytics can meet users: in the flow of work, at the moment a decision is being made, without forcing them to hunt for the right report first. As AI becomes part of everyday work, it’s critical to bring trusted Power BI data to where work gets done.  We’re in a phase of AI where systems can answer questions and help complete tasks for a user. But for business data, that only works when AI can reason over governed definitions instead of guessing from raw tables. That’s why we’ve invested in ensuring your Power BI data shows up everywhere you already work, starting with Microsoft 365 Copilot. And it works because of the thing your team has invested in for years: a well-modeled semantic layer."
