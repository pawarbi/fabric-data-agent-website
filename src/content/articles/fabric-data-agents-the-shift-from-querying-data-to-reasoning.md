The real shift is the move from human-authored to AI-authored logic, under guardrails.  **Before** Humans write explicit queries, design semantic models, orchestrate pipelines. Tools act as passive executors, doing exactly what they are told.  **Now** Humans declare the intent. Agents determine the plan (how to answer) and execute across governed data, semantic and orchestration layers."
