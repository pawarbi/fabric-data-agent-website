The Semantic Model is also optimized for Power BI Copilot, including the standalone version that functions as a SaaS MCP server for chatting on all of your content and data.  The dataset used is the publicly available [Medicare Part D Prescribers - by Provider and Drug](https://data.cms.gov/provider-summary-by-type-of-service/medicare-part-d-prescribers/medicare-part-d-prescribers-by-provider-and-drug), sourced from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).  🎥 Watch the full demo: YouTube Video https://youtu.be/2tLIGVZ4c8E"
